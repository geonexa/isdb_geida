# Home page images: project lifecycle

Six photos for the "From first idea to lasting impact" section. Save each as
`frontend/public/img/cycle/<key>.jpg` (landscape 3:2, about 2400 × 1600 px, under
~400 KB as JPEG quality 80). Until a file exists, the page shows a plain teal panel.

The question for each stage is overlaid at the bottom-left over a dark gradient, so
keep the lower-left third calm (sky, field, soft shadow), and keep faces and key
subjects in the upper or right part of the frame.

## Shared style (append to every prompt)

> Photorealistic documentary photograph, natural light, shot on a full-frame camera
> with a 35mm lens, realistic colours with a slightly muted earthy palette (ochre,
> sand, olive green, deep teal accents), gentle contrast, no text, no logos, no flags,
> no watermarks, no brand names, respectful and dignified depiction of people,
> setting in an Islamic Development Bank member country (Sahel, East Africa, Central
> Asia, Middle East or South Asia), 3:2 landscape, high detail, lower-left third of
> the frame calm and uncluttered.

## 1. Identification (PCN) — `pcn.jpg` — "Is this the right place?"

> High-altitude drone photograph looking down at a wide semi-arid river valley in the
> early morning: a meandering seasonal river, scattered villages, patchwork farmland
> on one bank, dry hills and a dirt road leading in. Soft low sun with long shadows,
> light haze, the sense of surveying a landscape before deciding where to build.

## 2. Preparation (PPRR) — `pprr.jpg` — "What is the starting point?"

> A small field team of two development professionals (a woman in a headscarf holding
> a rugged tablet, a man with a GPS receiver) talking with three villagers at the edge
> of a rural village; mud-brick houses, a water point and goats in the background.
> Late afternoon light, everyone engaged and listening, candid moment, positioned in
> the right half of the frame.

## 3. Appraisal and design (PAD) — `pad.jpg` — "How will success be measured?"

> A diverse team of four professionals around a large table in a bright, modern
> meeting room, studying a large printed satellite map of a river valley with
> boundaries drawn on it; a laptop shows a map and charts. Hands pointing at the map,
> notebooks, coffee cups, daylight from tall windows, focused collaborative mood.
> Shallow depth of field on the map.

## 4. Implementation (PIASR) — `piasr.jpg` — "Is it on track?"

> Aerial drone photograph of an infrastructure project under construction in a rural
> area: a new concrete irrigation canal being built alongside a road, an excavator,
> stacked materials and workers in high-visibility vests and helmets. Clear midday
> light, crisp detail, visible progress lines on the ground, surrounding farmland.

## 5. Completion (PCR) — `pcr.jpg` — "What was delivered?"

> Golden-hour aerial photograph of a completed irrigation scheme in use: a finished
> concrete canal running through vivid green fields of crops, small figures of
> farmers walking along the canal bank, a village at the edge, warm sunlight and long
> shadows, strong contrast between the green irrigated land and the dry land beyond.

## 6. Post-evaluation (PPER) — `pper.jpg` — "Did the benefits last?"

> Wide photograph of a thriving rural landscape years after a project: mature trees
> along a canal, healthy fields being harvested, a busy small market and a school
> building with children in the distance, a farmer in the foreground right looking out
> over the land. Soft evening light, calm and hopeful mood, sense of lasting change.
